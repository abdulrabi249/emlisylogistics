What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device (computer, tablet or phone) when you visit them. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners. Each cookie typically contains the name of the website that created it, how long it will persist, and a value — usually a unique number or short string of text.

Cookies are not programs, they cannot carry viruses, and they cannot access other data on your device. They simply allow a website to recognise your browser on return visits or to store a small piece of information between pages.

How to control cookies

Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You can usually find these options in the “Settings”, “Preferences” or “Privacy” section of your browser. Below are links to cookie management instructions for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data

For more detailed guidance on controlling and deleting cookies across all browsers and devices, visit allaboutcookies.org.

Please note that disabling or blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of this website. For example, if you block the cookie_consent cookie, the cookie banner will appear on every visit. If you block third-party cookies, the embedded Google Map on our contact page may not load correctly.
